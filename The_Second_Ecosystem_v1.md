5. The forum designs a personalized practice protocol from the 10,680 programs
6. Progress flows back to the Matrix (R2) to update the CEO's capability map

**What makes it work:** Anonymity. The forum never knows the CEO's identity. The CEO never knows the forum's members. The bridge is the data, not the people.

**What makes it work:** The AI has no ego. It does not prefer one forum's insight over another. It does not seek credit. It is the perfect echo chamber — because it is empty.
