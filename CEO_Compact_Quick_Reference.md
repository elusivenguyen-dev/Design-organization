#### THE WEEKLY LOOP (2 hours total)
```
Monday:    EXPERIENCE  → What happened? (30 min)
Tuesday:   REFLECT     → What did I learn? (30 min)
Wednesday: EXTRACT     → What principle emerged? (15 min)
Thursday:  APPLY       → How will I use this? (15 min)
Friday:    MEASURE     → Did it work? (15 min)
           + 15 min buffer
```

#### THE ULTIMATE TEST
> "If you were hit by a bus tomorrow, would your organization be better off because you were here?"

YES → System worked. You taught enough.  
NO → System failed. You hoarded knowledge.
